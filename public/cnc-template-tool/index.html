01 Drawing

No drawing loaded

Export one flat XY sketch at Z=0. Lines, arcs, circles, and closed polylines are supported. Convert splines in CAD. Dimensions, text, blocks, and nested islands must be removed.

02 Stock & zero

The drawing’s lower-left bounds sit at the margin from the stock’s lower-left edge. +X right, +Y up. All machining values use millimeters.

03 Cutter & cutting

Example values are not material presets. Use a center-cutting end mill and verify feeds, RPM, travel, and spindle control for your Genmitsu model. Maximum RPM must match your GRBL $30 setting.

04 Holding tabs

Evenly spaced tabs retain outside parts and hole waste slugs. Small holes fully swept by the cutter need no tabs. Other contours that cannot accommodate tabs are blocked. Preview tab locations and keep clamps outside every cutting path.

05 / Review & export

Load a DXF or try the sample.

Open the .nc file in Universal Gcode Sender on your Mac. Set the matching G54 zero, inspect the visualizer, run GRBL check mode ($C), then perform an air cut above the stock before cutting. Preview validates planned geometry; it cannot detect your clamps or machine setup.

What this version checks

Closed, non-crossing contours; outside/hole classification; tool-radius compensation; unreachable features within 0.03 mm; cutter-to-contour clearance; cutter envelope inside stock; safe retract height above the entered fixture height; pass depths and holding tabs. Arc approximation tolerance is 0.0025 mm.

Conventional milling with vertical plunges. Holes are contour cuts with retained slugs, not drilling cycles or pockets. Pockets, V-carving, and 3D STL machining are reserved for a later version.
