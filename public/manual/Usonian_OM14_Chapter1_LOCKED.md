## 1.1 Purpose

This manual documents the construction of the Usonian OM-14 steel string
acoustic guitar based on the supplied engineering drawings, refined
using modern lutherie practices and organized into a professional build
sequence.

Throughout this manual, all dimensions are presented using the following
standard:

-   **Metric dimensions (mm)** are the master dimensions.
-   **Imperial dimensions** are rounded to the nearest **1/16 inch**.
-   Unless otherwise specified, all measurements are finished
    dimensions.

Every dimension, material specification, and construction recommendation
has been normalized into a consistent format suitable for professional
workshop use.

## 1.3 Design Philosophy

The OM body size has earned a reputation as one of the most versatile
steel-string acoustic guitars ever developed.

Compared with a dreadnought, the OM offers:

-   Improved balance across all six strings
-   Greater articulation
-   Excellent fingerstyle response
-   Exceptional note separation
-   Comfortable seated playing position

The Usonian version preserves the classic OM proportions while
incorporating modern construction practices including:

-   Adjustable truss rod
-   Optional bolt-on neck
-   Precision machined neck geometry
-   Modern humidity tolerances
-   CNC-compatible templates
-   Improved serviceability

## 1.4 Tonewood Specifications

### Soundboard

Recommended species:

-   Sitka Spruce
-   Engelmann Spruce
-   European Spruce
-   Western Red Cedar

**Requirements**

-   Quarter sawn
-   Vertical grain
-   Minimum 20 growth rings per inch
-   Straight grain
-   No runout
-   Properly seasoned
-   Moisture content 6--8%

## 1.5 Structural Components

-   Neck Block: Vertical grain Honduras Mahogany, Maple, or neck
    species.
-   Tail Block: Mahogany, Maple, Spruce, or dense hardwood.
-   Kerfed Linings: Basswood, Mahogany, or Cedar.
-   Side Braces: Honduras Mahogany.
-   Bridge Plate: Hardwood with grain perpendicular to the soundboard.

## 1.6 Neck Assembly

### Truss Rod

Double-action adjustable.

            Metric     Fractional
  ---------------- --------------
    5.54 × 11.1 mm   3/16 × 7/16\"

Optional carbon fiber reinforcement may be used for thin necks, touring
instruments, or humid environments.

Bolt-on neck hardware: - Two brass connector bolts - 10 mm brass cross
dowels

Alternative: - 1/4-20 threaded inserts with stainless steel bolts

## 1.7 Decorative Materials

-   Body Binding: Solid wood preferred; Celluloid, ABS, or Ivoroid
    acceptable.
-   Purfling: Ebony, Maple, Rosewood, dyed veneers.
-   Heel Cap: Ebony preferred.
-   Headplate Veneer: Typically matches the back and sides.

## 1.8 Hardware

Recommended tuners:

-   Grover
-   Schaller
-   Gotoh
-   Waverly

acceptable for the nut.

Bridge pins: - Standard acoustic bridge pins. - Optional countersunk
installation.

Position markers: - Mother of Pearl.
