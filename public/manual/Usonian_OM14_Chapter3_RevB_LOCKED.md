# 3.1 Introduction

The quality of a guitar begins with the quality of its materials.

Careful wood selection and preparation establish the foundation for

stability, appearance, and tone. This chapter defines the material

standards and preparation methods used for the Usonian OM-14.

The Usonian OM-14 is designed around a small group of proven tonewoods.

Consistently selecting, inspecting, and preparing these materials

produces repeatable results while simplifying the building process.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3.2 Selecting Tonewoods

## Soundboard

-   Sitka Spruce (preferred)

-   Engelmann Spruce

Choose quarter-sawn stock with straight, even grain and minimal runout.

## Back & Sides

-   East Indian Rosewood

-   Black Walnut

-   Mahogany

Bookmatched sets should have similar color, grain, and density.

## Neck

-   Honduran Mahogany

-   Genuine Mahogany

-   African Mahogany

Select straight-grained stock free of knots and twist.

## Fingerboard & Bridge

-   Ebony

Use dense, stable stock free from cracks or checks.

## Bracing

Use quarter-sawn Sitka or Engelmann spruce with straight grain and high

stiffness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3.3 Moisture Content & Storage

All wood should acclimate to the workshop before machining.

**Target moisture content:** 6--8%

Store lumber:

-   Flat

-   Stickered when appropriate

-   Out of direct sunlight

-   In a climate-controlled shop (42--48% RH)

Reject or recondition material that falls outside the recommended

moisture range.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3.4 Lumber Inspection

Inspect every board before machining.

Verify:

-   Quarter-sawn grain where required

-   Minimal runout

-   No checks or splits

-   No bark inclusions

-   No insect damage

-   Minimal warp, cup, or twist

Bookmatched plates should exhibit good grain continuity and visual

symmetry.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3.5 Preparing Components

## Plates

-   Joint mating edges

-   Glue bookmatched halves

-   Thickness to specification

-   Mark centerline

## Neck Blank

Square the blank and mark centerline, headstock angle, heel, and truss

rod location.

## Fingerboard

Prepare oversize, slot before radiusing, and leave final taper until

later operations.

## Bridge

Mill flat and square. Leave oversize until saddle slot and pin locations

are complete.

## Brace Stock

Prepare straight-grained brace stock slightly oversize for final carving

after installation.

# Manufacturing Tips

-   Prepare slightly oversized blanks.

-   Reserve the straightest grain for the neck and braces.

-   Keep bookmatched sets together.

-   Photograph prepared components before construction begins.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Warnings

-   Do not machine wood outside the recommended moisture range.

-   Replace questionable material immediately.

-   Maintain identification marks until final assembly.
