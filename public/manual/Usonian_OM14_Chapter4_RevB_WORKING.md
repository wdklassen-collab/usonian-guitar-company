# 4.1 Introduction

The geometry of an acoustic guitar determines how every major component  
fits together. Accurate layout at the beginning of the build prevents  
cumulative errors later in construction and ensures the completed  
instrument plays and sounds as intended.

The dimensions and reference geometry established in this chapter are  
used throughout the remainder of the build. Before machining any  
component, establish the body centerline and verify all governing  
dimensions. Accurate layout at this stage minimizes cumulative errors  
and ensures proper alignment of the neck, bridge, soundboard, and back.

Unlike later chapters that describe fabrication procedures, this chapter  
defines the governing dimensions and reference geometry of the Usonian  
OM-14. Every measurement used throughout the build references the  
information established here.

The primary references established in this chapter are:

-   Body centerline  
-   Top reference plane  
-   Scale length  
-   Body outline  
-   Top and back radii  
-   Internal component layout  
-   Neck geometry

Once these references have been established and verified, every  
subsequent operation becomes significantly easier and more repeatable.

# 4.3 Body Reference Datums

Every measurement references four primary datums:

-   Datum A --- Body Centerline  
-   Datum B --- Top Reference Plane  
-   Datum C --- Scale Length  
-   Datum D --- Fingerboard Reference Plane

The fingerboard reference plane is verified by placing a straightedge on  
the fingerboard; it should project to the top surface of the bridge  
before the saddle is installed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Standard Orientation

Unless otherwise noted, all references to the guitar use the following orientation:

- Top – The soundboard side of the guitar.  
- Back (Bottom) – The back plate side of the guitar.  
- Neck End – The end of the body where the neck attaches.  
- Tail End – The end of the body opposite the neck, where the end graft and end pin are located.  
- Bass Side – The left side of the guitar when viewed from the front in the playing position.  
- Treble Side – The right side of the guitar when viewed from the front in the playing position.  
- Inside – The interior cavity of the guitar.  
- Outside – The exterior surface of the guitar.

Unless a procedure states otherwise, all descriptions assume the guitar is positioned Top-side up with the Neck End pointing away from the builder.

# 4.4 Body Outline

Transfer the body outline directly from the master template. Use it as  
the governing reference for the body mold, bending forms, plates, and  
routing templates.

Verify: - Upper bout - Waist - Lower bout - Neck block location - Tail  
block location - Body centerline - Overall symmetry

Correct any asymmetry before cutting the mold or body components.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4.5 Centerline Layout

Maintain the body centerline throughout construction. Verify it after  
joining plates, cutting the outline, installing blocks, fitting the  
neck, and locating the bridge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4.6 Body Curvature

Top radius: **30 ft**

Back radius: **15 ft**

Maintain both radii during sanding and fitting. Avoid flattening  
profiled surfaces.

# 4.8 Layout of Internal Components

Lay out all internal components before gluing braces.

-   Soundhole: 102 mm (4 in), centered.  
-   Rosette: concentric, leave material for leveling.  
-   X-brace: centered on body centerline.  
-   Bridge: verify scale, neck alignment, saddle position, fingerboard  
    projection, and temporary outside strings.  
-   Bridge plate: centered, grain 5–10° from perpendicular to top  
    grain.  
-   Tone bars and finger braces: locate from master template.  
-   Upper transverse brace: clear the soundhole and support the  
    fingerboard extension.

Complete the pre-bracing checklist before gluing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4.9 Neck Geometry

Verify neck centerline, neck angle, fingerboard plane, bridge  
relationship, scale length, and temporary outside-string alignment  
before permanent assembly.

A straightedge on the fingerboard should project to the top surface of  
the bridge before the saddle is installed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4.10 External Layout

Lay out the bridge footprint, fingerboard extension, binding and  
purfling ledges, end wedge, strap button locations, and optional  
pickguard after verifying neck geometry.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4.11 Geometry Verification

Verify:

-   Body symmetry  
-   Centerline  
-   Master dimensions  
-   Scale length  
-   Soundhole  
-   Internal layout  
-   Bridge location  
-   Neck geometry  
-   Top radius  
-   Back radius  
-   Rough stock

Correct any layout issues before assembly.
