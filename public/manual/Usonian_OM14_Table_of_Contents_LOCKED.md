# Chapters

## Chapter 1 --- Materials, Specifications & Component Tables

**Purpose:** Establishes the engineering foundation of the OM-14 with
materials, specifications, dimensions, tolerances, and reference tables.

## Chapter 2 --- Workshop, Tools & Jigs

**Purpose:** Covers workshop setup, required tools, jigs, fixtures, and
maintenance.

## Chapter 3 --- Wood Selection & Preparation

**Purpose:** Explains selecting, inspecting, milling, and preparing
tonewoods.

## Chapter 4 --- Body Geometry & Layout

**Purpose:** Defines body geometry, reference datums, scale length, and
layout procedures.

## Chapter 5 --- Building the Rim Assembly

**Purpose:** Covers side preparation, bending, blocks, linings, and rim
construction.

## Chapter 6 --- Soundboard Construction

**Purpose:** Documents preparation, bracing, rosette installation, and
voicing of the soundboard.

## Chapter 7 --- Back Construction

**Purpose:** Covers preparation, bracing, arching, and voicing of the
back.

## Chapter 8 --- Closing the Body

**Purpose:** Joins the body components into the completed shell.

## Chapter 9 --- Neck Construction

**Purpose:** Covers neck fabrication, truss rod installation, and
shaping.

## Chapter 10 --- Fingerboard Construction

**Purpose:** Covers slotting, radiusing, fretting, leveling, and
polishing.

## Chapter 11 --- Bridge Construction

**Purpose:** Covers fabrication and preparation of the bridge.

## Chapter 12 --- Neck Fitting & Final Assembly

**Purpose:** Establishes neck geometry and completes structural
assembly.

## Chapter 13 --- Binding & Purfling

**Purpose:** Installs decorative and protective trim.

## Chapter 14 --- Final Shaping & Surface Preparation

**Purpose:** Prepares the instrument for finishing.

## Chapter 15 --- Finishing

**Purpose:** Covers sealing, filling, finishing, curing, and polishing.

## Chapter 16 --- Hardware Installation & Final Setup

**Purpose:** Installs hardware and completes setup.

## Chapter 17 --- Final Inspection & Quality Control

**Purpose:** Documents inspection, troubleshooting, and final
acceptance.
